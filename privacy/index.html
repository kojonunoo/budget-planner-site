Privacy Policy

Monthly Budget Planner and Student Budget Planner · Effective 17 November 2026

We do not collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The apps have no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and they do not connect to the internet.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Monthly Budget Planner and Student Budget Planner apps for Android and iOS (together, “the app”), published by Kojo Nunoo. Both apps work the same way with your data.

What the app stores on your device

To work as a budget planner, the app saves what you enter in its private storage on your device:

  • accounts, transactions, categories and budgets
  • savings goals, bills, debts and recurring entries
  • receipt photos you choose to attach
  • your settings, such as currency, theme and reminder choices

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • It has no user accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not connect to your bank and cannot move or send money.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data collection.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your information.

Permissions the app asks for

Each permission is optional, and the app still works for budgeting if you decline it.

Photos
Used only when you attach a receipt. The app copies the photo you pick into its own storage and does not look at the rest of your library.
Notifications
Used for reminders you turn on, such as upcoming bills or budget limits. Reminders are created on your device and never show amounts.
Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint
Used only if you turn on app lock with biometric unlock. Your device checks your face or fingerprint itself; the app never receives biometric data.
Run at startup (Android)
Used to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.

Information you choose to share

You can export reports (CSV, Excel or PDF) and create backup files, then send them wherever you choose using your device’s share options.

  • Once a file leaves the app, the app you sent it to, or the person who received it, controls it.
  • Exported reports are not encrypted.
  • Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never see or store the passphrase, so we cannot recover it.

Security

Your records are kept in the app’s private storage and protected by your device’s own security. The database is not separately encrypted. You can add an app lock with a PIN and fingerprint or face unlock; the PIN is stored only as a secure hash in your device’s protected storage.

Device backups

On Android, the app turns off the system’s automatic app backup, so your records are not copied to a Google account. On iOS, your device’s iCloud or computer backups may include the app’s data, depending on your backup settings. Apple handles those backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your records in the app’s Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it has stored. Copies you exported or shared beforehand are not affected, because they are outside the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
